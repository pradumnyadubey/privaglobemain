Privacy Governance, DPIA and Training for Indian Organisations

Privacy compliance holds only when someone owns it. We help you set up the roles, decision rights, assessments and training that keep a programme running after the project ends.

Last reviewed: 8 November 2026 by the PrivaGlobe privacy team

Quick answer

Privacy governance is the set of roles, policies, reviews and reporting that keep personal data handled lawfully day to day. It includes impact assessments for risky processing, privacy by design in new projects and training for the people who handle data.

What we set up

  • Governance model: a privacy lead, a steering group, escalation paths and board reporting.
  • Policy set: a privacy policy, retention schedule, rights procedure, breach playbook and acceptable-use rules.
  • Impact assessments: a repeatable DPIA method, with templates and a risk score, for new systems, vendors and analytics.
  • Privacy by design: a short checkpoint in product, procurement and change management.
  • Training: role-based sessions for leadership, HR, marketing, customer support and engineers.

When is a DPIA needed?

Under DPDP, periodic Data Protection Impact Assessments are a duty of Significant Data Fiduciaries. Many other organisations run them voluntarily for high-risk projects, and the GDPR requires them in defined cases. We scope the method to your situation. See privacy risk assessment: a practical guide.

How do we keep it going?

We agree a yearly calendar of reviews, training and reporting. If you want an outside lead, DPO as a Service can run it with you. Read more in building a sustainable governance framework.

This page is general information, not legal advice. Laws and official dates change, so we confirm the current position for your situation at the start of every engagement.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a privacy officer?

A formal DPO is mandatory only for Significant Data Fiduciaries under DPDP. Every Data Fiduciary must publish a contact for privacy questions (Rule 9). Many companies still name an internal privacy lead.

Who should attend privacy training?

Everyone who touches personal data, with different depth by role. Leaders need accountability, engineers need design rules, and support teams need rights and breach basics.

Can you reuse our existing ISO 27001 controls?

Yes. We map existing security controls to privacy duties so you do not repeat work.
