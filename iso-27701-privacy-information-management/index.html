ISO 27701 privacy information management

ISO/IEC 27701:2025 preparation mapped to DPDP, by a consultancy that is not a certification body.

Quick answer

ISO/IEC 27701:2025 is a standalone, certifiable privacy management standard. We help you prepare and map it to DPDP duties.

Please note: PrivaGlobe is a consultancy. We help you prepare for certification. We are not a certification body and do not issue ISO certificates. Certification is awarded only by an accredited certification body.

Why consider it

ISO 27701:2025 is a certifiable Privacy Information Management System standard. It gives customers an outside check on how you manage personal data, across DPDP and other laws.

How it maps to DPDP

DPDP areaWhat a PIMS gives you
Notice and consentDocumented purposes and consent records
Rights requestsA defined procedure and response log
Breach responseTested incident procedure
VendorsProcessor controls and contracts

A PIMS is not a substitute for meeting the Rules. We use it as a structure to build and prove compliance.

Questions

Is ISO 27701 the same as ISO 27001?

No. 27001 is for information security. 27701 is for privacy information management.

Do I need 27001 first?

The 2025 edition of ISO/IEC 27701, published 14 October 2025, is a standalone certifiable standard. It no longer requires ISO 27001 as a prerequisite.

What happened to the 2019 edition?

It was withdrawn. Check with your certification body for current transition dates.
