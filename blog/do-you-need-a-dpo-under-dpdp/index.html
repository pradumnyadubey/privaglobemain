Do You Need a DPO Under DPDP? A Five-Question Decision Path

Find out in five questions whether DPDP requires a DPO for your business, and what to do if it does not.

Published 5 November 2026 · By PrivaGlobe privacy consulting team, Mumbai · General information, not legal advice

Quick answer

Only Significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint an India-based DPO under Rule 13. All other Data Fiduciaries must still publish contact details of a person who can answer privacy questions under Rule 9.

Every Data Fiduciary under the Rules has to publish contact details of a person who can answer privacy questions (Rule 9). A formal DPO under Rule 13 is only mandatory for Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDFs).

Five questions

1

Has the Central Government notified you as an SDF? If yes, you need an India-based DPO responsible to the Board. Stop here.

2

Do you handle large volumes of personal data, or sensitive data such as health or financial records?

3

Could your processing risk the rights of Data Principals, or touch sovereignty, electoral democracy, state security or public order?

4

Do you process children's data at scale?

5

Would a named, independent privacy lead help you win enterprise contracts?

The SDF test uses factors in Section 10 of the Act: volume and sensitivity of data, risk to rights, and the sovereignty, democracy, security and public-order points. If you answered yes to questions 2–4, prepare as if you may be notified. SDF duties include an independent auditor and periodic impact assessments.

What if the answer is no?

You still need a published contact and a working grievance process. Many mid-size companies name an internal privacy lead or hire DPO as a Service to avoid hiring full-time.

Penalty context. Breaching SDF obligations can attract up to ₹150 crore. Not publishing a contact falls under the general ceiling of up to ₹50 crore.

Questions

Is a DPO mandatory for every company?

No. A formal DPO is for Significant Data Fiduciaries. Everyone else must publish a contact for questions.

Can the DPO be outsourced?

An SDF's DPO must be based in India and responsible to the Board. Outsourcing can work if those conditions are met.

Who decides if we are an SDF?

The Central Government notifies SDFs using the Section 10 factors.
