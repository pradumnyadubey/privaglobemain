Data Breach Management and Regulatory Responsibilities

What Rule 7 expects after a personal data breach, and how to be ready in the first hour.

Published 8 November 2026 · By PrivaGlobe privacy consulting team, Mumbai · General information, not legal advice

Quick answer

Under Rule 7, a Data Fiduciary must tell affected Data Principals without delay and report to the Data Protection Board, with a detailed report within 72 hours. A rehearsed playbook is the best preparation.

First hour

1

Contain the incident and preserve evidence.

2

Notify your incident lead and privacy lead.

3

Assess what personal data is affected.

4

Decide whether it is a reportable breach.

5

Draft notices to the Board and affected people.

What the notice should say

  • What happened and when
  • What data was affected
  • Likely consequences
  • What you are doing and what people can do
  • Who to contact

Rule 6 also expects reasonable security safeguards and logs. Learn more about breach management and response planning.

Questions

How fast must we notify?

Affected people without delay, and the Board with a detailed report within 72 hours, subject to the Rules as notified.

Do vendor breaches count?

Yes. Your processor must alert you quickly so you can meet your own duties.

Should we rehearse?

Yes. A yearly tabletop exercise exposes gaps cheaply.
