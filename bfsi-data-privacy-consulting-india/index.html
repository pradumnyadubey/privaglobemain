Why privacy in BFSI is a layered problem

Banks, NBFCs, fintechs, insurers, brokers and asset managers already run mature security and compliance functions. The DPDP Act adds a consent-and-rights layer for personal data on top of sector rules, and many firms also handle data for customers in the EU, US or Middle East. The challenge is to build one control set that satisfies all of them.

Requirements that commonly apply

  • India's DPDP Act: notice, valid consent or a permitted ground, security safeguards, breach reporting and individual rights. Large processors may be notified as Significant Data Fiduciaries with extra duties. See DPDP compliance.
  • RBI requirements: including payment data storage rules, outsourcing and IT governance directions, and digital lending directions that limit how borrower data may be collected and used.
  • IRDAI and SEBI frameworks: information and cyber-security guidelines for insurers and cyber-resilience requirements for regulated market entities.
  • CERT-In directions: a short deadline for reporting specified cyber incidents.
  • GDPR, CCPA and other laws: for EU, US and other overseas customers and entities. See GDPR consulting and CCPA and CPRA.

Sector regulators update their rules regularly, so we confirm the current requirements for your licence type at the start of each engagement.

Frequently asked questions

Does the DPDP Act replace RBI or IRDAI rules?

No. It adds to them. Where sector rules are stricter or more specific, you follow both. We map the overlap so you can reuse controls.

What is a Significant Data Fiduciary?

A data fiduciary notified by the government based on factors such as volume and sensitivity of data and risk to individuals. It has extra duties, such as appointing a DPO based in India and carrying out audits and impact assessments.

How do we handle deletion requests alongside record-keeping duties?

Financial regulations often require retention for set periods. We define retention rules and a response approach that respects both.

Do we need to review our fintech and co-lending partners?

Yes. You remain responsible for personal data your partners process on your behalf, so agreements, due diligence and monitoring matter.

Can you help with customer or regulator audits?

Yes. We prepare evidence, answer questionnaires and support audit interactions.
