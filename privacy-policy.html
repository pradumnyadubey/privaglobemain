Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

PrivaGlobe ("we", "us") provides data privacy consulting, implementation and software. This policy explains what personal data we collect through www.privaglobe.com, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the notice requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and the EU/UK GDPR.

Who we are

PrivaGlobe, SP #125/C10, Halavpool, Kurla West, Mumbai - 400070, India.
Email: contact@privaglobe.com · Phone: +91 70210 17952

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through the contact form: your name, work email address, the regions you operate in, and your message. If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive the details you share in that conversation.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, prepare a proposal.
  • To keep a record of our communication with you.

We process this data because you asked us to contact you (consent under DPDP; consent or pre-contract steps / legitimate interest under GDPR). We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who receives it

Website usage data is processed by Google (Google Analytics). Contact form submissions are delivered to us by email through Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form-handling service that acts as our processor. Messages sent via WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms. We do not share your data with anyone else except where the law requires it.

Cookies, analytics and fonts

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (provided by Google) to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and from which country. Google Analytics sets cookies only if you click Accept on our cookie banner. If you decline or do not respond, the Google tag still loads but runs in a restricted mode that sets no analytics cookies and sends only limited, non-identifying signals. When you accept, it sets cookies and collects technical data such as your IP address, device and browser details and pages visited, which Google processes on our behalf and may transfer outside India and the EEA. We use this data only in aggregate to improve the site, not for advertising. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer, block these cookies in your browser settings or install Google's opt-out browser add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Fonts are hosted on our own server, so no separate font provider is contacted when you visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log technical data such as IP address for security and operation of the service.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and to maintain a reasonable business record of the conversation, and delete them when they are no longer needed or when you ask us to.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country, including outside India and the EEA. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to give you access to your personal data, correct or update it, erase it, restrict or object to its use, or withdraw your consent at any time. Under DPDP you may also nominate another person to exercise your rights if you are unable to, and use our grievance contact below. Under GDPR you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In India you may approach the Data Protection Board after first raising the matter with us.

To exercise any right, email contact@privaglobe.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and within any period the law sets.

Children

This website is for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to this website and the form service.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
