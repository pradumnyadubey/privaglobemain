Does the Saudi PDPL apply to your organisation?

The Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) was issued by Royal Decree and amended in 2023, with its Implementing Regulations and Regulation on Personal Data Transfers Outside the Kingdom. The Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) is the competent authority. The law applies to any processing of personal data that takes place in the Kingdom, and also to organisations outside the Kingdom that process the personal data of individuals residing in it. A company in India, the UAE or Europe serving Saudi customers can therefore be in scope.

Core PDPL obligations

  • Legal basis and consent: processing needs a lawful basis. Where consent is used it must be clear, specific and withdrawable.
  • Privacy notices: tell individuals what you collect, why, who receives it, how long you keep it and how to exercise rights.
  • Data subject rights: to be informed, access, request correction, request destruction and obtain a copy of their data, within the timelines in the Regulations.
  • Purpose limitation and minimisation: collect only what is needed for a defined purpose and keep it no longer than necessary.
  • Records and registration: controllers must maintain records of processing, and SDAIA's regulatory framework includes controller registration through its national platform where required.
  • Breach notification: notify SDAIA, and in some cases affected individuals, of incidents in the circumstances and timelines the Regulations set.
  • Cross-border transfers: transfers outside the Kingdom are allowed only on the grounds and with the safeguards the Transfer Regulations specify.
  • Data protection officer: some controllers must appoint a person responsible for compliance. See our DPO as a Service page.
  • Processor management: controllers remain responsible for processors and should have written agreements and oversight.

Penalties

The PDPL provides for criminal and administrative penalties. Disclosing or publishing sensitive data with the intent to harm an individual can lead to imprisonment and a fine. Other violations can lead to a warning or fines, with higher amounts for repeat offences. Regulators can also order corrective action. The exact penalty depends on the offence, so we explain the exposure for your specific activities.

Frequently asked questions

Does the PDPL apply if we have no office in Saudi Arabia?

It can, if you process the personal data of individuals residing in the Kingdom. We confirm this in an applicability review.

Who is the regulator?

The Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) is the competent authority responsible for the PDPL.

Can we transfer Saudi personal data to India?

Transfers outside the Kingdom are permitted only on the grounds and with the safeguards set out in the Transfer Regulations. We map your flows and identify which ground and safeguards fit.

Do we need a Data Protection Officer?

Some controllers must appoint a responsible person under the Implementing Regulations. We check whether this applies to you and can provide the role as a service.

Can you prepare documents in Arabic?

We prepare the core documents in English and can arrange Arabic versions of notices and consent language.
