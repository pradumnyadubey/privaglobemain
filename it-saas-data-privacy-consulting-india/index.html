Data Privacy Consulting for IT, SaaS and Technology Companies

Technology companies are both Data Fiduciaries and Data Processors, and enterprise buyers ask hard questions. We build lean programmes that answer them.

Last reviewed: 8 November 2026 by the PrivaGlobe privacy team

Schedule a consultationRequest a DPDP assessment
Quick answer

IT and SaaS companies usually act as processors for client data and as fiduciaries for their own staff and users. They need processor contracts, security evidence, privacy by design in product and a clear answer to customer due-diligence questionnaires.

What technology companies usually need

  • Role mapping: where you are a fiduciary and where a processor, product by product.
  • Customer contracts: data processing terms and sub-processor lists that stand up in procurement.
  • Due-diligence answers: a maintained pack for security and privacy questionnaires.
  • Product privacy: notices, consent, deletion and access in the application.
  • Cross-border work: transfers for clients in the EU, UK, US and Gulf, see GDPR and CCPA.
  • Standards: alignment with ISO 27001 and ISO 27701 where buyers ask for them.

Right-sized for growth

A ten-person start-up and a global services firm need different programmes. We scale the work, then add controls as customers and regulators expect more.

This page is general information, not legal advice. Laws and official dates change, so we confirm the current position for your situation at the start of every engagement.

Frequently asked questions

We are only a processor. Does DPDP still affect us?

Yes. Your clients will pass obligations down by contract, and you may be a fiduciary for your own staff and users.

Can you help with security questionnaires?

Yes. We build and maintain a reusable answer pack and evidence library.

Do customers need ISO certification from us?

Some do. We prepare you for the assessment, but certification itself is issued by an accredited certification body.
