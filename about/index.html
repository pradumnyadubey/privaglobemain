About PrivaGlobe: Global Data Privacy Consulting from Mumbai

PrivaGlobe helps organisations meet privacy laws across India, Europe, the United States, Asia-Pacific and the Middle East with one team and one control framework.

Book a consultationWhatsApp us
Quick answer

PrivaGlobe is a data privacy consultancy based in Mumbai. We provide consulting, implementation, documentation, audits, DPO as a Service and privacy software for DPDP, GDPR, CCPA, PDPA and PDPL, and we both advise and deliver.

What PrivaGlobe does

We help organisations understand which privacy laws apply to them and turn those laws into working controls. Our work covers six areas: consulting (gap assessments, roadmaps and board briefings), implementation (data mapping, consent and rights workflows, vendor management and breach response), documentation (policies, notices, records of processing, DPIAs and agreements), audit (independent readiness and vendor audits), DPO as a Service and software that keeps evidence, requests and risks in one place. You can buy one module or the whole programme.

Where we work

We serve companies in five regions, each with its own law and regulator:

  • India: the DPDP Act and Rules
  • Europe and the UK: the GDPR and UK GDPR
  • United States: CCPA and CPRA
  • Asia-Pacific: the PDPA of Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand
  • Middle East: Saudi Arabia's PDPL

How we work

Our delivery model is the same in every country, so quality does not depend on where you operate. We map where personal data lives and flows, assess gaps and risks by law, design governance, policies and controls, deliver workflows and training, operate the programme through monitoring, requests and incidents, and improve it using analytics. We map overlapping requirements once and add local steps only where a law demands them.

Standards we align with

We help clients prepare for assessments and align controls with recognised frameworks, including ISO 27701 (privacy information management), ISO 27001 (information security), ISO 31000 (risk management) and SOC 2 (trust services reporting). Alignment with a framework is not certification, and we say so plainly in every engagement.

How we keep our guidance accurate

Privacy law changes often. Each guide on this site shows the date it was last reviewed, and we re-check dates, thresholds and penalties against official sources. Browse our privacy resources or the glossary to see the standard we hold ourselves to.
