Questions we hear most

Does the DPDP Act apply to my company?

If you process digital personal data in India, or offer goods or services to people in India, it very likely does. Our applicability assessment confirms your obligations and your role.

Can we buy just one service?

Yes. Consulting, implementation, documentation, audit, DPO services and software are all available individually or as one programme.

We operate in several countries. Do we need separate projects?

No. We map overlapping requirements once and apply a single control framework, adding local steps where a law demands them.

Do you only advise, or will you implement too?

Both. We advise where you want direction and implement where you want delivery, so recommendations turn into working controls.

How long does a DPDP programme take?

It depends on your size and data landscape. After a short assessment we give you a phased plan with clear milestones.
