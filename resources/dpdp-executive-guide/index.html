DPDP Implementation: An Executive Guide for Business Leaders

A short, plain-language briefing for boards and senior leaders.

Last reviewed: 8 November 2026 by the PrivaGlobe privacy team

Schedule a consultationRequest a DPDP assessment
Quick answer

The main DPDP obligations and penalties apply from 13 May 2027, with penalties up to ₹250 crore. Leaders need to name an owner, fund a gap assessment, approve a roadmap and review progress every quarter.

1. Why this is a board matter

DPDP applies to any organisation handling digital personal data of people in India. Duties sit with the Data Fiduciary, which is the organisation, not the IT team. Penalties up to ₹250 crore apply for the most serious failures, such as not taking reasonable security safeguards.

2. Key dates

DateWhat happens
13 November 2025Rules notified
13 November 2026Consent manager framework starts
13 May 2027Main obligations and penalties apply

3. Ten questions every leader should be able to answer

  1. Are we a Data Fiduciary, and could we be a Significant Data Fiduciary?
  2. Who owns DPDP compliance?
  3. Where does personal data sit, and who are our processors?
  4. Do our notices and consent meet Rule 3?
  5. Can we respond to rights requests on time?
  6. Do we have security safeguards and logs (Rule 6)?
  7. Can we meet breach reporting duties (Rule 7)?
  8. Do we delete data when we should (Rule 8)?
  9. Do we handle children's data, and if so with parental consent (Rule 10)?
  10. What do we tell the board each quarter?

4. A 90-day plan

Days 1-30

Appoint an owner. Map data and vendors. Run a gap assessment.

Days 31-60

Fix notices and consent. Publish a contact. Draft the breach playbook.

Days 61-90

Update vendor contracts. Train staff. Rehearse a breach. Report to the board.

5. Budget and ownership

Plan for assessment, remediation, tooling where needed and an ongoing owner. Many mid-size firms use DPO as a Service or a monthly advisory retainer instead of a full-time hire. See pricing.

6. What to report quarterly

  • Gap closure against the roadmap
  • Rights requests closed on time
  • Open high risks and vendor reviews
  • Training coverage
  • Incidents and response times

Frequently asked questions

What should the board ask first?

Who owns DPDP, where personal data sits, and how far we are from the Rules. A gap assessment answers all three.

How much will this cost?

It depends on size, systems, vendors and laws in scope. Our DPDP consulting starts from ₹50,000 per month, confirmed in writing after scoping.

Do we need a DPO?

Only if notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary. Everyone else must publish a privacy contact.
