DPDP Gap Assessment: Find What to Fix Before 13 May 2027

A structured review of how your organisation handles personal data today, measured against the DPDP Act and Rules, ending in a prioritised plan your teams can act on.

Last reviewed: 8 November 2026 by the PrivaGlobe privacy team

Schedule a consultationRequest a DPDP assessment
Quick answer

A DPDP gap assessment compares your current notices, consent, security, retention, rights handling, vendor contracts and breach response with what the DPDP Act and Rules require, then ranks the gaps by risk. The main obligations and penalties apply from 13 May 2027.

What does the assessment cover?

  • Applicability and role: whether you are a Data Fiduciary, whether you may be notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary, and which processors you use.
  • Data flows: what personal data you collect, where it is stored and who it is shared with.
  • Notice and consent (Rule 3): whether notices are clear, itemised and linked to a lawful purpose.
  • Security and breach response (Rules 6 and 7): safeguards, logging and the ability to report on time.
  • Retention (Rule 8) and rights (Rule 14): erasure schedules and how requests are received and answered.
  • Children's data (Rule 10): verifiable parental consent where it applies.
  • Vendors: contracts and oversight of processors.

What do you receive?

  • A scored gap report with a heat map by area.
  • A prioritised remediation roadmap with owners and target dates.
  • A short board briefing so leadership can approve budget and ownership.
  • An evidence list showing what you will need to demonstrate readiness.

How does it work?

1

Scoping call to confirm laws, systems and teams in scope.

2

Document review and interviews with business, IT, HR and legal.

3

Scoring against the Rules and a ranked gap list.

4

Roadmap workshop and handover. Remediation support is optional.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a gap assessment take?

It depends on your size, number of systems and vendors. We confirm the timeline after a short scoping call and agree it in writing.

Can we do the assessment ourselves?

Yes, the readiness check and checklist help you start. An independent assessment adds an outside view, consistent scoring and a plan the board can sign off.

Does the assessment include remediation?

It does not have to. You can take the roadmap and deliver it internally, or ask us to support the fixes under a monthly engagement.
