ISO 27001 consulting for DPDP security readiness

ISO 27001 preparation that doubles as evidence of DPDP security safeguards.

Quick answer

ISO 27001 is an information security standard. It is not required by DPDP, but an ISMS covers the Act's security safeguards and gives auditable proof.

Please note: PrivaGlobe is a consultancy. We help you prepare for certification. We are not a certification body and do not issue ISO certificates. Certification is awarded only by an accredited certification body.

How ISO 27001 supports DPDP

The DPDP Rules require reasonable security safeguards such as encryption, access control, monitoring and one year of logs. An ISMS built to ISO 27001 covers these, with auditable proof.

What our preparation covers

  • Scope and risk assessment
  • Statement of Applicability across the 93 Annex A controls
  • Policies, internal audit and management review
  • Support during the certification body's audit

Questions

Is ISO 27001 mandatory in India?

No, but many enterprise and BFSI buyers ask for it, and it is strong evidence of reasonable security safeguards under DPDP.

How many controls are in ISO 27001:2022?

Annex A has 93 controls, grouped into organisational (37), people (8), physical (14) and technological (34).

Do you issue the certificate?

No. An accredited certification body does. We prepare you for the audit.
