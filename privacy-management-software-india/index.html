What is privacy management software?

Privacy management software helps an organisation run its data protection programme day to day. Instead of spreadsheets and email threads, teams keep records of processing, consent, requests, assessments and incidents in one system, with an audit trail that regulators and customers can review. It does not replace legal advice or governance, but it makes them repeatable.

Modules that work alone or together

  • Data discovery and mapping: find and classify personal data across systems, vendors and processes, and keep records of processing current.
  • Risk and impact assessments: run PIAs and DPIAs, score risk and track remediation to closure.
  • Consent and cookie management: capture, honour and withdraw consent across websites and apps, with an audit trail.
  • Rights request handling: intake, verification, search and response for access, correction, erasure and portability requests, with deadline tracking.
  • Breach management: incident log, assessment, notification timelines and lessons learned.
  • Vendor risk: vendor register, assessments, agreements and renewal reminders.
  • Reporting and monitoring: executive dashboards, evidence and alerts when regulations or your processing change.
  • Training and awareness: role-based privacy training with completion records.
  • Cross-border transfers: transfer mapping, assessments and contract tracking.

Built for several laws at once

One record of processing and one request queue can serve India's DPDP Act, the GDPR, the CCPA and CPRA and the Saudi PDPL. The platform tags each activity to the laws that apply and surfaces where one law needs an extra step, such as a different request deadline or a transfer assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need software to comply with the DPDP Act or GDPR?

No law requires a specific tool. Software helps once your volumes of requests, vendors and processing activities make manual tracking error-prone. We tell you honestly whether you are at that point.

Can the platform handle several laws?

Yes. Activities, requests and assessments can be tagged to DPDP, GDPR, CCPA, PDPL and other laws so one record serves all of them.

Can we use it without your consulting services?

Yes. Modules are available on their own. Many clients add documentation, audit or DPO support later.

Where is data stored?

Hosting location is agreed with you based on the laws that apply and your own policies. We confirm this during requirements.
